FAQ

Is This Guide Official?

No. The guide is maintained by students. USAAIO publishes the competition rules, dates, registration, and syllabus.

Do I Need Advanced Math Before Starting?

Algebra and some Python is enough to start. Linear algebra, probability, and calculus each get their own modules, and you can pick them up when a later topic needs them.

Should I Learn TensorFlow or PyTorch?

PyTorch. The syllabus names it for deep learning tasks, so that is what the guide uses once you are past NumPy and scikit-learn.
